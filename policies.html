Payment & Billing Policy

All proceeds from our product catalogue directly support artisan livelihoods, river clean-up machinery, and environmental drives. We maintain full financial transparency across all transactions.

Accepted Payment Methods:

  • UPI (Unified Payments Interface): Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM and all bank UPI apps.
  • Debit & Credit Cards: Visa, MasterCard, RuPay, and American Express.
  • Net Banking: Secure transfer across all major public and private scheduled banks.
  • Cash on Delivery (COD): Permitted on domestic orders up to ₹5,000. Higher value orders require prepaid digital settlement for transit security.

GST Compliance: Sales are billed under GSTIN: 20ABHCS8922N1ZZ at the statutory 5% rate for handicrafts. A formal GST invoice is generated with each order and downloadable from your account.

Payment Security: Transactions comply with RBI 2-Factor Authentication guidelines. SwachhataPukare Foundation never records or stores CVV codes or payment PINs.

03 · Fulfilment

Shipping and Delivery

Product prices shown in the shop are in INR and exclude print and courier charges. A courier estimate is transparently calculated in the cart and at checkout.

PAN India Partner Network: Delivery is handled through our national logistics partners (National express couriers, India Post Speed Post, and Shiprocket network), providing verified coverage across 28,000+ PIN codes across all 28 states and 8 union territories.

Dispatch & Transit: Orders are dispatched within 24 to 48 business hours. Delivery typically takes 3 to 5 business days depending on location. Free shipping is unlocked on all orders above ₹999.

04 · Returns

Returns and Refunds

If your handcrafted item arrives damaged in transit, report it with a photograph within 48 hours of delivery to hello@swachhatapukare.com or via WhatsApp for an immediate free replacement or refund.

Because pieces are made by hand, slight natural variations in texture and weave are natural characteristics. Non-damaged items may be returned within 7 days in their original unused packaging.

05 · Order Changes

Cancellation Policy

Orders may be cancelled before dispatch by contacting our support team or via WhatsApp. Once dispatched, regular return procedures apply. Approved refunds are credited back to the source payment method within 5–7 banking days.

06 · Data Privacy

Privacy Policy & Data Protection (DPDP Act, 2023)

SwachhataPukare Foundation complies with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act). We act as a responsible Data Fiduciary.

Lawful Purpose & Consent: Personal details collected during checkout (name, delivery address, phone number, and email) are processed strictly for order fulfillment, logistics dispatch, and mandatory tax invoicing. At checkout, explicit consent is required before processing.

Your Rights: Under the DPDP Act, you have the right to access a summary of your personal data, request correction of inaccurate data, or request erasure of your customer profile. Write to our designated Data Grievance Officer at privacy@swachhatapukare.com.

No Commercial Sharing: We do not sell, rent, or monetize your contact or browsing data with third-party advertisers.
