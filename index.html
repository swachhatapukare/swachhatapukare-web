Material story

From the river to your desk

Water hyacinth grows thick in water bodies. The Foundation works with it as a raw material — harvested, prepared and woven into everyday pieces that carry a Water Hyacinth tag in the catalogue.

Only the pieces genuinely made from it wear that tag. Everything else is catalogued simply as handcrafted.
